## FAQ

**How does the savings estimate work?**
The audit engine compares your reported spend and seat counts against published vendor pricing for each plan. If your Cursor seat count exceeds your team size, or you're on a Business plan with two people, those gaps have a specific dollar value. All math is deterministic and based on the plan prices listed on each vendor's pricing page. Nothing is AI-generated — Gemini is only used to write the executive summary paragraph after the numbers are computed.

**Is the audit free?**
Yes, running an audit is free. The full report (recommendations, savings breakdown, executive summary) requires an email to unlock — so you can get the report link sent to yourself.

**How do you handle our data?**
The audit only uses what you enter: which tools, which plans, how many seats, how much you're spending. We don't connect to billing systems, vendor APIs, or anything external. Nothing is verified against actual vendor accounts.

**What if we only use one or two tools?**
The audit still runs. For small stacks, the main findings tend to be plan-tier alignment (are you on the right plan for your team size?) rather than cross-tool overlap. Some audits come back clean — the product will say so explicitly rather than fabricating recommendations.

**Why not just use a spreadsheet?**
You could. The audit takes about the same amount of time to fill out as building a spreadsheet from scratch. The difference is the output: a formatted, shareable report with specific recommendations and a governance summary your finance or ops team can actually read. If you'd rather do it in a spreadsheet, that's genuinely fine — the product is for people who'd rather not.

## Copy Notes / Decisions

- Avoid "AI-powered" in any headline — it's noise and undersells the deterministic angle
- "Defensible" is a word that resonates with the finance/ops audience; use it where appropriate
- The "60 seconds" claim is accurate for simple stacks (3–5 tools); mention it only in places where the onboarding form is visible so it's credible
- Don't use the word "optimize" — it's overused in this space and signals AI-generated vagueness
- "Shareable report" is probably the most underutilized value prop; should appear early and often
